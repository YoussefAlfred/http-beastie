001 — About

The Person
Behind the Numbers

My name is Youssef Gohar, and I arrived at the American University in Cairo with a scholarship, a gym bag, and a notebook full of half-solved integrals. Freshman year. Everything is new, and somehow nothing surprises me.

I earned a Full Excellence Scholarship — the kind that tells you the work you put in during high school actually meant something. It also means I have absolutely no excuse not to perform.

I chose Engineering. Not because I stopped loving mathematics — in fact I never did. But I wanted my equations to leave marks on the physical world. Structures. Systems. Things you can point at. Still, late at night, pure math pulls me back like gravity.

When I am not in class or buried in problem sets, I am almost certainly on a field, a court, a track, or in a pool. Sport is not a hobby for me — it is a second language.

003 — Passion

Mathematics
as a Way of Seeing

The Obsession
"Pure mathematics is, in its way, the poetry of logical ideas."
From number theory to differential equations, I approach math the way others approach art — with admiration, compulsion, and the quiet joy of finding something beautiful in something abstract.
The Contradiction
Engineering was not the "safe" choice — it was the brave one.
I could have studied pure mathematics. Instead I chose to carry that love into a discipline that demands results. Every proof I've ever admired now has a deadline, a material, and a load-bearing constraint.
Favorite Areas
Calculus · Linear Algebra · Combinatorics · Complex Analysis
I find combinatorics meditative and calculus visceral. Complex analysis feels like discovering a secret room inside a house you already thought you knew perfectly well.
The Goal
Build things that only someone who loves math would think of.
Engineering and mathematics aren't opposites. The most elegant bridges, chips, and algorithms in history were made by people who refused to separate the two. I intend to be one of them.

004 — Journey

So Far, So Good

2007 — Cairo, Egypt
Born Into a City That Never Stands Still
Cairo shaped my pace. A city of 20 million teaches you to think fast, adapt faster, and find beauty in organized chaos — which, incidentally, is also a decent description of multivariable calculus.
2015 — First Sport
Football, Then Everything Else
Started with football, ended up never stopping. Every new sport felt like a new language I already somehow half-knew. Competed regionally in football and swimming by secondary school.
2019 — Secondary School
Mathematics Becomes Personal
A teacher handed me a problem no one else in the class could solve. I stayed up until 2 a.m. and found a solution using a method we hadn't been taught yet. That night I understood what it felt like to think.
2024 — AUC Admission
Full Excellence Scholarship, Engineering Track
Awarded the Full Excellence Scholarship. Enrolled in the School of Science and Engineering. Showed up on campus with high expectations and at least three unresolved debates about whether to minor in math.
Now — Freshman Year
In Progress
Attending AUC. Taking courses that challenge and excite me. Playing sport whenever the schedule allows. Solving problems — in all the senses of that phrase.
